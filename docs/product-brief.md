# Product brief

## Product

Money Wise is a simple personal finance tracker for people who want a clear
monthly answer to the question: "Where did my money go?"

## Target user

The target user is someone who wants to record everyday income and expenses
without using a complicated accounting system.

## Problem

A user may know how much money entered and left their account, but not which
categories or individual purchases explain the total.

## Goal

Make it easy to enter transactions and understand monthly income, expenses,
remaining difference, and category spending.

## First-version boundary

The first version covers monthly summaries and transaction management. It does
not include accounts, recurring transactions, authentication, currencies
other than ISK, exports, or category budgets.
